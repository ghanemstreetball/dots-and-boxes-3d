Privacy Policy for Dots & Boxes 3D Effective date: November 12, 2026 Dots & Boxes 3D ("the Game") respects your privacy. This policy explains information processed when you play the Game, use online multiplayer, view advertisements, purchase an optional subscription, or contact support. You can play without creating an account. 1. Information processed Game information stored on your device: The Game uses local device or browser storage for player names, language and game preferences, achievements, game statistics, and information needed to resume an online match. This information is generally kept on your device unless you choose to use features that transmit it. Online multiplayer: If you play online, the Game uses PeerJS/WebRTC signaling and connectivity services, including STUN and Cloudflare TURN. These services may process network information such as IP addresses, connection identifiers, and technical connection data. Player names and game state may be exchanged between connected players. Network services may process information necessary to establish or relay a connection. Feedback and support: If you submit feedback, we receive the message, any email address you provide, your player name, and diagnostic details included with the submission, such as game settings, device/browser information and submission time. Feedback is transmitted using Web3Forms and delivered to our support inbox. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback. Advertising: The Android version uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from IP, and other data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, depending on device settings, consent, and applicable law. See Google's Privacy Policy. Purchases: If you choose the optional ad-removal subscription, purchases and subscription status are handled through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may process transaction and account information under its own policies. 2. How information is used We use information to operate and improve gameplay, save settings and progress, connect online players, troubleshoot issues, respond to feedback, display and measure ads, manage subscription access, and protect the Game from abuse. 3. Third-party services The Game integrates or relies on Google AdMob and Google Play services, PeerJS/WebRTC connectivity, Cloudflare TURN, and Web3Forms. The web version is hosted through GitHub Pages and may load external software resources. These providers may process technical information under their own privacy policies and terms. Their practices are not controlled by this policy. 4. Data sharing We do not sell feedback messages or player-submitted contact information. Information may be shared with service providers as necessary to provide the features described above. Online opponents receive gameplay information needed for a shared match. Advertising partners may process data as described in Section 1. 5. Retention and choices Locally stored game information generally remains until you clear the Game's app data or browser storage, or uninstall the Game, subject to device behavior. You can choose not to submit feedback or use online multiplayer. Advertising preferences and permissions may be managed through your device and Google settings. To request access to or deletion of feedback that you sent us, contact the support email below. Third-party providers maintain their own retention practices. 6. Children's privacy The Game does not require an account. Advertising and online features may involve third-party processing. Children should use the Game with appropriate parental guidance. Please do not submit children's personal information through feedback. Parents or guardians may contact us regarding privacy concerns. Any additional child-directed advertising and consent requirements will be handled according to applicable law and platform policies. 7. Security We use service providers and technical measures intended to support the Game, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. 8. International processing Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable law. 9. Changes to this policy We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when we publish revisions. 10.
