How to Play, Rules & How to Win

1. How to Play (Drawing Lines) Players take turns connecting two adjacent horizontal or vertical dots. Tap directly on the dark groove between two dots, or tap one dot and then tap its neighbor to place your glowing 3D beam.
Tip : "hint" is available across all game sizes and modes (1× per player by watching an ad)
2. Game Rules (Capturing Boxes & Extra Turns) Completing the 4th wall of any 1×1 square claims that box with your 3D X or O monument (+1 point) and immediately grants a Bonus Turn!
3. How to Win & Winning Strategy Once every box on the grid is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins! Pro Tip: Avoid drawing the 3rd wall of a box when possible so your opponent cannot easily claim the 4th wall.
